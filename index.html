Medicare Part D Prescriber Anomaly Detection

Python · SQL · DuckDB · Spark · scikit-learn · MLflow · Databricks

Peer-relative anomaly detection on 1.2 million Medicare Part D prescribers a year, using an Isolation Forest checked against a simple peer z-score rule. Without fraud labels, the flags are validated against later exclusions from Medicare by the HHS Office of Inspector General. In 2023 data, prescribers in the top 1% were about four times as likely to be excluded later, and the forest beat the baseline at every cut-off. Every filter and join is logged with record counts, and the scoring method was fixed before each year's results were seen.
